The cloud sounds harmless... until a giant data centre moves next door. Now, some neighbours are fighting back. Why?

Because the AI boom needs real buildings. Real land. And a whole lot of resources. This growth has a catch.

The International Energy Agency projects data centres will use roughly DOUBLE the electricity in twenty thirty compared with twenty twenty-five.

Now zoom in on your street. Can the grid handle it? And who gets the bill for those new power lines?

Then comes cooling. Some systems use water. How much? That depends on the design and location. In dry regions, that matters.

And some neighbours worry about the NOISE. Cooling equipment. Backup generators. Right beside their homes.

This is already a real argument. From Mexico to South Africa, communities have demanded answers about power, water, and nearby development.

But wait. Data centres also power useful services. Supporters point to investment and tax revenue. Those benefits belong in the conversation, too.

So here is the test. Show the resource use. Control the noise. Explain who pays. And involve residents BEFORE construction starts.

The cloud might be global. Its neighbours are local. So if the future moves next door... who gets a say?
